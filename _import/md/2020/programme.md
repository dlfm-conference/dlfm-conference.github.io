## DLfM 2020 Programme

</div>

<div class="field field-name-field-content field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden">

<div class="field-items">

<div class="field-item private-show even">

The conference will be held between 9am and 3pm EDT, on Friday 16 October 2020, hosted by the Schulich School of Music, McGill University, Montréal.

The conference will take place on Zoom, with supported discussion activities on [Slack](http://ismir2020.slack.com). Registered participants will receive an e-mail with links and more details by the end of Wednesday 14 October.

Times below are all EDT.
