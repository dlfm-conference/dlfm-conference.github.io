#### Registration Details (DLfM 2024)

There are two categories for registration:

- **Author registration - €104 - Deadline May 13th:  **Must be paid by one author per paper, preferably the presenting author. Other attending authors must either register for the *DLfM attendee (non-IAML)* category below, or register to attend IAML. 
- **​​​​​DLfM attendee (non-IAML) - €15- Deadline June 17th**:  This is for DLfM-only attendees. IAML attendees have free access to DLfM sessions—register for IAML [here](https://iaml2024.sun.ac.za/registration/).
