The conference will be held on Thursday 28 July 2022 in Prague association with IAML 2022

All sessions (papers and posters) will be at the [Municipal Library](https://www.iaml2022.cz/congress-venue/municipal-library-of-prague). Paper presentations will be given in Congress room B.
