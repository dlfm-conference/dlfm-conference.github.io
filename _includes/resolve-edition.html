{%- comment -%} Resolves which edition a page represents, for co-rendering the current year at both `/` and `//`. - A year page (e.g. 2026/index.md) sets `year` in its front matter → it IS the source: `edpage` = itself. - A root stub (e.g. index.md) sets no `year` → it represents the CURRENT edition: `edpage` = the year page with the same `role` and `year == site.current_year`. Sets: `year` (int), `ystr` (string key for _data lookups), `edpage` (source page), `edition` (the _data/editions.yml entry). {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.year -%} {%- assign year = page.year -%} {%- assign edpage = page -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign year = site.current_year -%} {%- assign edpage = site.pages | where: "role", page.role | where: "year", year | first -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign ystr = year | append: '' -%} {%- assign edition = site.data.editions | where: "year", year | first -%}
