{%- include edition-context.html -%} {%- comment -%} The nav describes ONE edition: the archived one while reading an archived page, otherwise the current one. So "Programme" and "Proceedings" always mean the year on screen, never a silent jump to a different edition. Items come from that edition's own `pages:` list in _data/editions.yml, ordered by _data/page_labels.yml. A year without a Programme page therefore has no Programme link (2014–2017), and a year with extra pages gains them (2025 offers Registration, Venue and Local arrangements). Three deliberate exceptions keep the archive from becoming a trap: · the LOGO always points at "/" — one constant, conventional route back to the current edition from anywhere in the archive; · "Home" points at the edition being navigated (/2019/), so that edition is coherent to browse; · on small screens the first menu entry is an explicit way out, because the archive band that carries that link on wide screens is hidden there. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign nav_edition = site.data.editions | where: "year", site.current_year | first -%} {%- assign nav_prefix = '' -%} {%- assign home_href = '/' -%} {%- if is_archived -%} {%- assign nav_edition = archive_edition -%} {%- assign nav_prefix = '/' | append: page.year -%} {%- assign home_href = nav_prefix | append: '/' -%} {%- endif -%}
