{%- comment -%} Resolves the EDITION CONTEXT of the current page, for site chrome (header, archive bar, body class). Content pages use resolve-edition.html instead — that one also resolves the source page, and needs `role`. Sets: is_archived — true when this page belongs to a past edition archive_edition — that edition's _data/editions.yml entry (only when archived) A page belongs to a past edition when it declares a `year` in front matter EARLIER than site.current_year. Root stubs (/proceedings/ etc.) declare no year and co-render the current edition, so they are never archived; nor are year-less pages such as /about/ and /previous/. Strictly `<`, not `!=`: a chair may hand-create next year's pages before the rollover Action flips current_year, and a future edition is not an archive. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign is_archived = false -%} {%- if page.year and page.year < site.current_year -%} {%- assign is_archived = true -%} {%- assign archive_edition = site.data.editions | where: "year", page.year | first -%} {%- endif -%}
