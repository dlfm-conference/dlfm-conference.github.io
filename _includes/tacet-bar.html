{%- comment -%} A bar of music that does not sound: the DLfM logo's own bass clef, five staff lines, one whole rest, closed by a final barline and marked *tacet* — the instruction in a part that is silent. Used by /404.html. Purely decorative, hence aria-hidden: whatever page includes this must carry the meaning in its own text, and a screen reader loses nothing by skipping it. Geometry, in staff units — 10 to a staff space, lines at y = 3, 13, 23, 33, 43: · no opening barline, because a system does not start with one; it starts with the clef, which is what makes the figure read as music at a glance; · the whole rest hangs FROM the fourth line from the bottom and is half a space deep, reaching the middle of the space below it, centred on the measure after the clef; · the final barline is the engraver's thin-then-thick pair. Everything is a filled rect or path, never a stroke: a stroked line straddles its own coordinate and goes soft at fractional scales, a filled rect does not. The clef path was traced from assets/logo/dlfm-logo.png by tools/trace_clef.py — the logo's own clef rather than a look-alike, so the two cannot drift apart. Re-run that script if the logo is ever redrawn. {%- endcomment -%}
